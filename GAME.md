# Puzz

A puzzle adventure in a house full of puzzles, in the tradition of *The 7th
Guest*, *The 11th Hour* and *The 13th Doll*: you explore a strange house room
by room, and each room's curiosities are puzzles to solve, which open the way
on.

But **bright and light-hearted instead of scary**: a sunny, toy-like,
cel-shaded house (the look of Schnau), warm colours, playful characters, and
humour in place of dread. A whimsical house, not a haunted one.

The house, characters, story and puzzles are Puzz's own; those games are the
genre we're working in, not something to copy.

To be designed: the house and its host, the story that strings the rooms
together, the puzzles (logic, sliding, pattern, word and number puzzles, set
into the furniture), how you move around, and how hints work.
